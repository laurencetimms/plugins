# The weekly brief

## Before you write

1. Read the user's interest profile. Note their background, lenses, followed threads (and what each already covers), declined topics, delivery preferences, and the brief log.
2. Don't repeat items offered in recent briefs unless there is something genuinely new.
3. Research the last seven days, or the time since the last brief in the log. Aim for 12–20 searches across:
   - each followed thread
   - each of the user's lenses
   - the under-covered zones in `subject-ai.md`
   - one or two deliberate searches for critics and affected people
4. Fetch every page you will cite.

## Structure

Readable in about five minutes. Depth belongs in the deep dive, not here.

**Subject line (if emailed):** `AI analyst brief — <date>`

1. **The shape of the week.** Two or three sentences on the pattern, not a list.
2. **Your threads.** For each followed thread, only what is genuinely new, why it matters, and the caveats. If nothing is new, say so in one line.
3. **The menu.** Five to seven numbered items. Each has:
   - one line on what it is, marked **[headline]** or **[under-covered]**
   - one line on why it might matter
   - a disclosure if Anthropic or Claude is involved
4. **Outside your lanes.** Zero to two items that clear the bar in `method.md`, with the criteria named.
5. **How to reply.** Tell the user how to pick items, for example "Open a chat with Claude and say 'AI analyst: 2 and 5', or 'less of X'". If the brief is emailed, say that replies to the email won't reach the analyst. Link the interest profile.
6. **Sources.** Links, grouped by item.

## Delivery

Use the delivery the profile asks for, falling back in this order:

1. **Email** to the user's own address, if this session can send email. Use simple HTML with inline styles: a serif body at 16px, headings, a numbered menu, links. Send only to the address in the profile.
2. **A document** with the date in the title, if this session can create documents. Link it from the profile's brief log.
3. **The chat**, as the reply.

## After sending

Add one row to the top of the profile's brief log: the date, a short list of the menu items offered, and "Awaiting reply". Change nothing else in the profile.
