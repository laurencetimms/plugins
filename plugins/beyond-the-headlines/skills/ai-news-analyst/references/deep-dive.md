# Deep dives

When the user picks menu items ("AI analyst: 2 and 5") or asks to dig into a story.

## Before you write

1. Read the interest profile, and find the brief the items came from in the brief log. If you can't tell which items the numbers refer to, ask.
2. Research properly: at least eight searches per item, primary sources wherever possible, and deliberate searches for critics and for the people affected.
3. Work through all seven questions in `method.md`.

## Structure

Write it in the chat unless the user asks for a document. Aim for something that can be read in ten minutes.

1. **What "the story" is.** One headline often covers several distinct things. Separate them.
2. **What the people closest to it are arguing about.** This is often not what the headline is about.
3. **Known, unknown and conjecture.** Keep these clearly apart.
4. **The human angle.** Who is affected and how. Label your interpretation.
5. **What to watch.** Specific signals that would change the assessment.
6. **Sources.**

## Afterwards

1. Ask whether to add the story to their followed threads.
2. Update the profile:
   - In the brief log, record what they picked.
   - If they want to follow the story, add a thread with what the deep dive covered, their questions in their own words, and what to watch for.
